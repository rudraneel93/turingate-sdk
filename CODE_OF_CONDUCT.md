# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Turingate to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Good behaviour includes:

- being kind and respectful, including when you disagree;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully;
- focusing on what is best for the people who use the project;
- owning up to mistakes and learning from them.

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks;
- sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances;
- publishing someone's private information without their permission;
- trolling, spam, or deliberately disruptive behaviour;
- anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and the Hugging Face dataset and Space) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@rudraneel93](https://github.com/rudraneel93), through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/rudraneel93/turingate-sdk/security/advisories/new) (use it for conduct reports too) or the contact details on the GitHub profile. Every report is reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban anyone who does so.
